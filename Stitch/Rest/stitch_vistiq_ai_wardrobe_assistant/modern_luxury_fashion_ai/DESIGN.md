## Brand & Style

The visual personality combines the understated restraint of modern luxury fashion curation with the warmth and intuitive ease of top-tier consumer lifestyle applications. It deliberately strips away algorithmic noise, prompt jargon, and cold tech aesthetics in favor of a personal shopper’s refined editorial touch.

### Core Character & Atmosphere
- **Approachable Luxury:** Sophisticated and uncluttered without feeling sterile, intimidating, or overly clinical.
- **Editorial Calm:** Generous breathing room, deliberate negative space, and a measured visual hierarchy that mirrors tactile, high-end lookbooks.
- **Quiet Intelligence:** System-driven outfit recommendations and style metrics manifest as subtle confidence indicators rather than complex analytical charts.
- **Warm Tactility:** Soft background surfaces, gentle elevation, and pill-shaped touchpoints create an inviting, physical feel across every screen.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy, museum-grade canvas that lets apparel photography and silhouette compositions command immediate attention.

- **Primary (`#111827`):** Rich charcoal black utilized for dominant actions, primary text hierarchies, and structural icon accents. Avoids pure black (`#000000`) to preserve an organic, fabric-like finish.
- **Secondary (`#059669`):** Soft botanical emerald used sparingly for AI confidence badges, match scores, sustainability verifications, and affirmative micro-states. Accompanied by an ultra-light wash container (`#ECFDF5`).
- **Tertiary (`#6B7280`):** Balanced graphite slate applied to secondary metadata, supporting labels, muted icons, and passive navigation items.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9FAFB`):** A soft, low-glare off-white foundation for screen surfaces, setting up a distinct contrast layer beneath pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Surface Borders (`#E5E7EB`):** Fine, hairline definitions that gently separate structural regions without visual weight.

## Typography

Typography relies on a dual-sans structure pairing Plus Jakarta Sans for titles with Inter for all informative, reading, and interface layers.

- **Headline Hierarchy:** Rendered in sentence case with tight tracking to evoke the poise of an independent fashion publication. Never use harsh all-caps styling on headings.
- **Body & Product Metadata:** Handled entirely by Inter to maintain crisp glyph distinction across clothing specifications, pricing, fabric descriptions, and styling notes.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Compact and purposeful. Stylist tips and match percentages utilize medium or semibold weights with slight tracking adjustments to anchor clarity at diminutive sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible 12-column grid anchored to a maximum container width of `1320px`, pairing visual density with breathing space.

### Column Systems & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `3rem` (48px) margins. Promotes an asymmetrical 2-column split (e.g., 5-column conversational context and curated lookbook alongside a 7-column product breakdown).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Stacks complex side-by-side modules into balanced vertical flows.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) margins. Employs horizontal swipe rails for lookbook variations to maintain a compact vertical footprint.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Internal component spacing follows 4px and 8px base units. Interactive cards and product containers use generous internal paddings (`space-md` to `space-lg`) to preserve a modern luxury feel.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical sheet-layering: pristine white cards elevate cleanly against the `#F9FAFB` ground through warm, ultra-diffused drop shadows and crisp perimeter boundaries.

### Layering Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** Soft neutral `#F9FAFB`, completely flat.
- **Resting Cards & Containers:** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting directly above the canvas, bounded by a 1px border of `#E5E7EB` combined with a whisper-light ambient drop shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`.
- **Hovered State / Interactive Cards:** Elevated depth transitioning to `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.06), 0 4px 8px -3px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)` alongside a border transition toward `#D1D5DB`.
- **Floating Modals, Drawers & Popovers:** Elevated overlay depth: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04)` over a dimming scrim of `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The shape system leverages balanced, soft geometries that humanize apparel cards and micro-interactions.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Standard inputs, compact drop-downs, and inline utility buttons.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Primary product cards, outfit boards, and conversational style blocks.
- **Feature & Focal Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Hero showcase cards, style moodboards, and bottom-sheet panels.
- **Full Radius (`rounded-full`):** Category pills, AI match badges, tags, user avatar rings, and floating action triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#111827` fill, pure white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) or soft-radiused (`0.5rem`), with a subtle inner edge highlight. On hover, background shifts to `#1F2937` with an elevated shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E5E7EB`, text `#111827`. Hovers reveal a subtle surface transition to `#F3F4F6`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or resting fill, text `#111827`. Hover applies a `#F3F4F6` background tint.

### Chips & Badges
- **AI Match Indicator:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#059669`, border 1px solid `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`. Fully rounded capsule (`rounded-full`) incorporating a subtle emerald gem dot or percentage value.
- **Filter / Category Pill:** Height 36px, `rounded-full`, text `#4B5563`, resting background `#FFFFFF` with `#E5E7EB` border. Selected state triggers a solid `#111827` fill with crisp white text.

### Cards & Collections
- **Product Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, `1rem` corner radius, 1px border `#E5E7EB`. Imagery uses an aspect ratio of 3:4 or 4:5 with an off-white background fill (`#F3F4F6`). Includes integrated quick-save bookmark buttons positioned with floating inset padding.
- **Outfit Lookboard Card:** Grouped product cluster rendered on `#FFFFFF` with clear internal grouping lines and subtle badge overlays for contextual styling rationale.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Conversational Style Bar:** Floating pill-shaped input (`rounded-full`), white background, framed with `#E5E7EB` and ambient shadow. Left icon anchored by subtle typography prompt hints; right action features a compact circular `#111827` submit button.
- **Standard Field:** Height 44px, radius `0.5rem`, border `#E5E7EB`, text `#111827`, placeholder `#9CA3AF`. Focus state provides a clean `#111827` border with a 3px ring of `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.08)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular and soft-square controls with a 1.5px border in `#D1D5DB`. Checked state displays a solid `#111827` center with a clean white tick mark.

### Interactive Split Curation
- Left rail accommodates fluid, conversational wardrobe curation or style direction, while the right pane presents a dynamic, clean-column lookbook grid showcasing matching luxury items, accessories, and fit recommendations.
